What "automating check-in" actually means for small hosts

You don't need a smart lock, a property management system or a $50/month software subscription. For most hosts with one to five properties, automating check-in means:

  • Guests get all arrival info without you sending it manually each time
  • No more "Where do I pick up the key?" messages at 9pm
  • A professional first impression — even if you're renting your first apartment
  • Works on any platform: Airbnb, Booking.com, VRBO, direct bookings

2
Get your guest link and QR code You receive a single URL like checkin-card.com/?id=abc123. This is what you share with every guest — the same link works every time.
3

4
Done — update anytime Changed the WiFi password? Updated the entry code? Edit your guide once and every future guest sees the new info automatically. No need to re-send anything.

What your guests actually see

When a guest opens the link on their phone, they see a clean, mobile-friendly page with:

  • Check-in time, check-out time and address with a Google Maps button
  • Step-by-step entry instructions (key safe, smart lock, reception or meet-host)
  • WiFi name and password — with a QR code they can scan to connect instantly
  • House rules and useful info about the property
  • Nearby places: supermarket, pharmacy, transport, restaurants — added automatically
  • A WhatsApp or phone button if they need to reach you

✅ No app download needed. Guests open the link in any browser on any phone. Nothing to install, no account to create.
